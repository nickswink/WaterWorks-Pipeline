Privacy Policy

How we handle information you share with us.

WaterWorks Pipeline Co. respects the privacy of our customers and website visitors. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

We use your information to provide water service, respond to inquiries, send required public notices and water-quality information, and maintain the security of our systems. We do not sell customer information.
