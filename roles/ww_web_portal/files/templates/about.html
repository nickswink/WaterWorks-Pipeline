About the Cedar Ridge System

How water gets from the Cedar River to your tap.

WaterWorks Pipeline Co. is a regional water wholesaler that owns and operates the Cedar Ridge Regional Transmission System. Water travels from source to customer through a chain of facilities engineered for reliability and water quality.

The system, end to end

  1. Cedar River Intake (PS-1). Raw water is drawn from the Cedar River at our intake pumping station and screened before transfer.
  2. Cedar Ridge Water Treatment Plant. Water is clarified, filtered, and disinfected. Chemical feed systems adjust pH with caustic soda (NaOH) and apply chlorine (Cl₂) for residual disinfection.
  3. 38-mile transmission system. Finished water moves through our regional transmission mains toward the higher-elevation service areas.
  4. Hilltop Booster Station (PS-3). Booster pumps lift pressure for the Summit pressure zone and feed the elevated storage.
  5. Summit Elevated Tank. A 1.0-million-gallon elevated tank stabilizes pressure and provides storage for peak demand and fire protection.

Communities we serve

Through our member towns, the Cedar Ridge system delivers wholesale water to four communities across the region, supporting homes, schools, businesses, and fire protection throughout the service area.

Our commitment

Since 1962, our team of operators, treatment specialists, and field technicians has worked around the clock to keep safe water flowing. We invest continuously in our treatment processes, transmission mains, and monitoring systems to protect public health and system reliability.
