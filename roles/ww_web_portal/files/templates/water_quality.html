Water Quality

Transparency about what's in your water.

Every year we test thousands of water samples for regulated and unregulated contaminants. Our water meets or exceeds all state and federal drinking water standards. The results are summarized in our annual Consumer Confidence Report (CCR), also called the Annual Water Quality Report.

Common questions

Why is my water sometimes cloudy?

Cloudy or milky water is usually trapped air and is not a safety concern. Let a glass stand for a moment and it should clear from the bottom up.

What causes occasional discoloration?

Routine main flushing or nearby hydrant use can stir up naturally occurring mineral sediment. Run cold water for a few minutes to clear it.

Is my water hard?

Cedar Ridge water is moderately hard. Contact Customer Service for the current hardness figures for your service area.
