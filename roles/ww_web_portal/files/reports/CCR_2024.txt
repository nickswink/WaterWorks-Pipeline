 2024 CONSUMER CONFIDENCE REPORT (ANNUAL WATER QUALITY REPORT)

Is my water safe?
  Yes. Your tap water met all state and federal drinking water standards during
  2024. This report summarizes the monitoring results for that year.

Where does my water come from?
  Source water is drawn from the Cedar River at Intake Pumping Station PS-1,
  treated at the Cedar Ridge Water Treatment Plant, and delivered through our
  regional transmission system to member communities.
