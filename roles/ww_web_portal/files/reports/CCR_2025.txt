Is my water safe?
  Yes. Last year, as in years past, your tap water met or exceeded all state
  and federal drinking water standards. This report summarizes where your water
  comes from, what it contains, and how it compares to the standards.

Where does my water come from?
  Source water is drawn from the Cedar River at Intake Pumping Station PS-1,
  treated at the Cedar Ridge Water Treatment Plant, and delivered through our
  38-mile regional transmission system to member communities.

Treatment summary
  - Coagulation, sedimentation, and filtration
  - pH adjustment using sodium hydroxide (NaOH)
  - Disinfection using chlorine (Cl2); a disinfectant residual is maintained
    throughout the distribution system

Lead information
  If present, elevated levels of lead can cause health problems. WaterWorks
  Pipeline Co. is responsible for providing high quality drinking water but
  cannot control the materials in home plumbing. Flush your tap 30 seconds to
  2 minutes before using water for drinking or cooking if it has been sitting.
